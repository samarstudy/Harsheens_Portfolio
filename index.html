Copywriter by profession, professional overthinker by choice.

While everyone warned me against turning my passion into my career, I decided to do exactly that! As an observant person, I pick up on details that others often miss and transform them into ideas that connect with people.

I believe the smallest observations often lead to the biggest ideas, and that's where my creativity thrives. Curious by nature and eager to keep learning, I approach every challenge as an opportunity to grow.
